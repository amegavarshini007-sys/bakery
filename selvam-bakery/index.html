Our Story

A Family Name Manali Has Trusted Since Day One

Selvam Sweets & Bakery is one of the most reputed sweet shops in Manali, Chennai. For more than 25 years, we've served our neighbourhood with fresh sweets, snacks, bakery products, cakes, beverages, hot chips and customised orders — always with quality, hygiene, reasonable pricing and friendly service at the centre of everything we do.

Our owner, Mr. P. Anand, has built a reputation among local families for his honesty and genuine care for customer satisfaction — the same values that guide every tray that leaves our kitchen today.
